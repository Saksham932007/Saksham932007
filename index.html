Research

What does observability really cost?

Preprint · submitted to Software: Practice and Experience, Oct 2026

Cardinality Before Contention

A factorial benchmark of per-command observability on a concurrent Rust/Tokio key-value server: 29,520 runs on 14 Azure VMs across 100–3,000 clients and 3 workloads, using randomized interleaved trials and run-clustered confidence intervals.

The finding: metric cardinality mattered more than lock contention. Every table and figure is reproducible from the repository. Not yet peer reviewed.
